Generally speaking, the job search has always been somewhat of a **struggle**. If you haven't been living under a rock for the past few years, you've probably heard of this game-changing tech stack called *LLMs*.

Now, I don't hate LLMs, but it's undeniable that they've brought a mix of positive and negative effects. I've experienced firsthand the shrinking of the software engineering job market, especially for early career and new grads. After all, why hire a whiny human (me, in this case) who will occasionally *argue* when you can just prompt an agent to write the code?

---

Well, plot twist: I landed a new job! 

I'm officially joining **Verizon Connect** as a **Machine Learning Engineer**, with a focus on **Edge AI**, **Embedded Systems**, and low(er) level **systems programming**.

I am **extremely** excited to get started (in fact, today was my very first day!) and dive into new challenges.

See you in a while, crocodile!

Stay tuned, stay hungry, and most of all stay systems!
